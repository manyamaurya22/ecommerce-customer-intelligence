Business insights (from the current filter)

    Methodology & data limitations

    • Revenue = quantity × unit_price × (1 − discount). Profit = revenue − quantity × unit_cost. Orders = distinct order_id. AOV = revenue ÷ orders. Currency symbol is display-only.
    • Repeat purchase rate = customers with ≥ 2 distinct orders inside the filtered window ÷ customers in that window.
    • RFM segments are computed once on the full loaded dataset (reference date = last order + 1 day), so a customer keeps the same segment under any filter. Recency score: 3/2/1 by tercile (most recent = 3). Frequency score: 1 order = 1, 2–3 = 2, 4+ = 3. Monetary score: tercile of lifetime revenue. Champions: R=3 and (F=3, or F=2 with M=3). Loyal: F≥2, R≥2. New: R=3, one order. At Risk: R=1, F≥2. Hibernating: everyone else.
    • Cohorts group customers by first order month within the filtered rows; Mk = share of the cohort ordering k months later. Blank = month not yet observed.
    • Insights are rule-based descriptions of the displayed numbers plus hypotheses to test. They are not forecasts and carry no predictive-accuracy claim.
    • Limitations: the sample is synthetic (seeded random generator) and represents no real company. Terciles on small or tied data can be coarse; no returns, shipping, taxes, or marketing cost; cohort figures get noisy for small cohorts; filtering by segment hides customers' orders in other segments' definitions only by design, not recomputation. All processing happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.
